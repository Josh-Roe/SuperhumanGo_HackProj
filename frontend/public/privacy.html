Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. This event organizer website is a hackathon project. It helps organizers plan an event, review invitations, and send them through their own Google account.

What we collect

  • Your account email, used to sign you in.
  • The event details you enter: title, time, location, invitee addresses, and accessibility facts.
  • If you connect Google: an OAuth token for your account, stored encrypted on our server.
  • If you connect Canva: an OAuth token for your Canva account, stored encrypted on our server.
  • RSVP responses from your invitees. Dietary and accessibility details stay private to the organizer and are never put in shared invitations.

How we use Google data

  • Calendar (calendar.events): to create and update the event on your calendar and read invitees' response statuses.
  • Gmail (gmail.compose): to create invitation and change-notice drafts and send them only after you approve them in the app.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): to read replies in the invitation thread so RSVPs can be recorded, and to confirm whether a message was already sent so it is never sent twice.

We use Google data only to provide these features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

Event data is stored in Supabase. Each organizer's events are visible only to that organizer. OAuth tokens and server credentials are kept on the server and never sent to the browser.

Removing your data

You can revoke this app's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your account and events deleted, email ethanc8858@gmail.com.
